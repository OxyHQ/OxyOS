# ADR 0065: The session ending because of a problem

Status: **accepted; implemented** (the heartbeat and the worker's watch, the login program's record of the end, the login screen's line, the session's report and notification, Resume and its question, the scenarios). Decided on 10 November 2026, at the platform author's request for a Mac's "nothing ever breaks" (the research behind it: a hung session was a hung machine, and nothing came back after one). Builds on [ADR 0020](./0020-oxy-account-login) (the login program and its workers), [ADR 0061](./0061-crashes-and-resetting-an-application) (reports, notifications, the crash sheet), [ADR 0064](./0064-sleep-restart-shut-down-and-log-out) (Reopen windows, the power key's inhibitor) and [ADR 0054](./0054-recovery-and-erasing) (Safe Mode).

## Problem

The session (`oxyos-session`) is the compositor, the launcher and the supervisor in one process. If it crashed, every application ended with it (they read the compositor's hangup) and the login program's worker treated its end as a sign-out: the login screen came back saying nothing, no report was written, and the next login opened nothing, since `reopen-at-login` was written only when the person asked for an end. If it hung, nothing watched it: no input, no Dock, no lock screen, and, since the session holds logind's `handle-power-key` inhibitor (ADR 0064, decision 7), not even the power key's short press. Only the firmware's four-second hold got out.

A Mac's window server is watched by `watchdogd`: one that stops checking in is ended, the person is back at the login window, and after logging in they are told it quit unexpectedly. Its Resume brings the same applications back after a forced restart or a power cut, and when an application quit while reopening its windows, it asks before trying again.

## Decision

1. **A heartbeat to the worker that started the session** (`oxyos_seat::heartbeat`).
   - The worker (`oxyos-login --worker`, the session's parent) binds a datagram socket in the person's runtime directory (`$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/oxyos-heartbeat-<worker>`, theirs alone, mode 0600), which the session is told by `OXYOS_HEARTBEAT` and takes out of its environment first, so nothing it starts is told it; the worker removes it once the session ended. No descriptor crosses the session's start, so no `unsafe` is needed on either side.
   - The session says it is alive (`.`) as each turn ends, at most every half second, and its turn waits at most two seconds (`heartbeat::WAIT`) while it has a heartbeat; sent without blocking, so a full socket drops a beat, never the turn. As it ends in order it says so (`E`).
   - The worker arms on the first beat (the session's start has its own deadlines, ADR 0009) and holds the session hung after ten seconds without one (`heartbeat::HANG`), 45 once it said it is ending (its applications' time to quit). Its clock is the monotonic one, which stands still while the machine sleeps, so a suspend is never a hang. The compositor's longest real stalls (the ThinkPad's: 1.3 s for an HDMI hot-plug's modeset, 340 ms on every display wake) are far inside ten seconds; a span that could pass them would be announced on the same socket, and none does today.
   - Hung, the worker sends the session `SIGABRT` (and `SIGCONT`, for a stopped one), so its core and report are written as a crash's, and its process group `SIGKILL` three seconds later if it has not ended. The logind session closes, the login screen comes back, and the power key's inhibitor goes with the process.
   - The login screen's session is watched the same way: a hung login screen is ended and started again (ADR 0061, decision 6).
2. **The end is said.**
   - Before it exits, the worker tells the login program how the session ended (`FromWorker::Ended`: its exit status, the signal that ended it, hung, or asked to end; and how long it ran). An end that was a problem (a status other than 0, a signal nobody sent on the login program's behalf, a hang) is kept by the login program (`Manager::session_ended`).
   - **The login screen** says so, quietly under its field: *Your session ended because of a problem.* (*Tu sesión se cerró por un problema.*). The login program starts the login screen's session with `OXYOS_LAST_SESSION_ENDED=problem`; the login screen asks its session once it is shown (`{"lastSession": {}}`, its `shell` grant's, no input needed, answered `{"lastSession": {"problem": bool}}`), so it never misses a word told before it listened. It says it until someone signs in.
   - **The account's next session** is told how its last one ended (`OXYOS_SESSION_ENDED`: `signal 11 3600 1762790000`, how, the seconds it ran, when). As the person, never root writing into their home, it writes the session's report into their crash reports (`so.oxy.session_<time>.crash`, ADR 0061's form: *Your session ended unexpectedly.*, its reason, how long it ran, the system) and posts the session's notification, *Your session ended unexpectedly* (*Tu sesión se cerró inesperadamente*), saying *OxyOS reopened your apps.* when Resume did, with *Report…*; the notification itself or its *Report…* shows the report in the crash sheet. A session that ended as asked, or by the person signing out, says nothing.
3. **Resume: what ran is opened again after any end** (`oxyos_compositor::resume`).
   - The session keeps a record in the person's state folder (`$XDG_STATE_HOME/oxyos/session`, JSON, written whole as it changes and never synced on the turn): the applications it launched that run, by registry id, once each, in launch order (a file chooser is none); those it reopened as it started; and whether it ran a minute.
   - At the next login: the person's own end (Log Out, Restart, Shut Down) is decided as ADR 0064 decided it, by its `reopen-at-login`; **any other end** (a crash, a hang, a power cut, a kernel panic, the machine stopped from elsewhere) reopens what ran, when the person's latest choice of *Reopen windows when logging back in* is on, as a Mac's "the most recent choice applies" after a forced restart.
   - Reopened after the person's login items, never twice; never at the login screen; in Safe Mode nothing is opened, and what runs there is what the next start reopens.
4. **Asking before reopening again.** An end that was not asked for within a minute of a start that reopened applications may have been caused by them: the next login asks first, the session's system sheet `{"kind": "reopen-again", "applications": [NAME]}` drawn by the shell: *The last time you logged in, OxyOS quit unexpectedly while reopening your apps.* *Do you want to try to reopen them again?*, the applications listed, **Reopen** (the keyboard on it) and **Don’t Reopen**. Spanish: *La última vez que iniciaste sesión, OxyOS se cerró inesperadamente al volver a abrir tus apps.*, *¿Quieres intentar volver a abrirlas?*, *Volver a abrir*, *No volver a abrir*. Don’t Reopen opens nothing and leaves nothing to reopen.
5. **One set of decisions.** The session and the development compositor make the same ones: `Resume::at_login` and `Resume::serve`, `Crashes::session_ended`, `login::answer_last_session`.

## Tests

- **The heartbeat** (`oxyos-seat`): armed only by a first beat; hung only past its limit; a 3 s stall between beats never a hang; an ending session given its time; the session beating at most every half second and never blocking on a full socket; a socket that cannot be reached no heartbeat.
- **The worker** (`tools/oxyos-login/src/worker.rs`, stand-in sessions run by `sh`, their beats sent from the test on the worker's socket): a session that beats then goes silent is sent `SIGABRT` and ends by it, said hung; one that ignores `SIGABRT` is killed three seconds later; one stopped (`SIGSTOP`) is ended too; six beats 0.4 s apart then an exit is no hang; one that never beats is never held hung, and its `SIGSEGV` and exit status 1 are said; one the login program ends was asked.
- **The channel and the manager**: every end crosses; a crash, a hang and a failure are problems, signing out and being asked are not; the login screen says it until someone signs in, a wrong password changes nothing, only the account's own next session is told, once; the login screen's and the session's arguments carry it.
- **Resume** (`oxyos-compositor`): what ran reopened after a crash or a power cut; the person's latest choice applying; the person's own end decided by its reopen file; an end within a minute of reopening asking, and Don’t Reopen leaving nothing; a minute's run no crash of its reopening; a first start with no record keeping the reopen file; an unreadable record as none.
- **The shell and the login screen** (`apps/shell/tests/resume.test.ts`, `apps/login/tests/lastSession.test.ts`): the question's words, buttons and keyboard in English and Spanish; the login screen's question and only a problem said.
- **Scenarios**: `login-session-ended` (the login screen says it, read by the assistive technology, and signing in goes on as always); `session-resume` (Inbox reopened once after a crash, the notification with *OxyOS reopened your apps.* and Report…, the report's reason `SIGSEGV`, the record following the session); `session-resume-question` (the question, the keyboard on Reopen, Don’t Reopen opening nothing); `session-resume-question-reopen` (Return reopens Inbox once).

## Not in this decision

- **Applications surviving the compositor.** Splitting the supervisor from the compositor so applications reconnect to a new one (KDE Plasma 6's `kwin_wayland_wrapper`) is a long project of its own.
- **The session's own backtrace in its report.** The core `SIGABRT` makes is systemd-coredump's to keep ([ADR 0068](./0068-crash-evidence-and-kernel-panics)); the report says how the session ended.
- **Windows' places.** What reopens is applications; each restores its own windows as it does when opened (a decision of its own).
- **Remembering a problem across the login program's own restart.** The login program keeps the end in memory; a power cut or a kernel panic is Resume's and the kernel's to tell.
