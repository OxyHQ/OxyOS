# ADR 0064: Sleep, restart, shut down and log out

Status: **accepted; implemented** (protocol version 38, the scene, the session's part over logind, the C ABI, the Bloom host, `@oxyos/platform`, the shell and the login screen; the scenarios run in the gate). Never tried on a real machine's power: every test and scenario asks a played logind. The owner: "faltan botones para poder apagar/reiniciar/etc como en macOS". Builds on [ADR 0006](./0006-seat) (logind), [ADR 0009](./0009-session-shell) (the shell), [ADR 0010](./0010-lock-screen) (the lock, sleep held for it), [ADR 0020](./0020-oxy-account-login) (the login screen, signing out), [ADR 0021](./0021-menu-bar) (the top bar's menus), [ADR 0024](./0024-system-shortcuts) (keys the system takes), [ADR 0025](./0025-hardware-keys-and-control-center) (hardware keys), [ADR 0030](./0030-installing-applications) (the system's sheets), [ADR 0033](./0033-protocol-versions) (version 38), [ADR 0057](./0057-battery-and-energy) (logind's `Suspend`, the played services) and [ADR 0058](./0058-the-platform-api) (`@oxyos/platform`'s `application`).

## Problem

Nothing in OxyOS turned the computer off, restarted it or ended the session but Settings' Sign Out… and the keymap's Ctrl+Alt+Backspace. The top bar's Oxy menu had System Settings, Applications, Show Desktops and Lock Screen. The login screen had no buttons at all. And the power key was logind's: its default `HandlePowerKey=poweroff` turned the machine off at once, with every application's unsaved work in it, since nothing in the session held logind's `handle-power-key` inhibitor.

A Mac has, in the Apple menu: Sleep, Restart…, Shut Down…, Lock Screen (⌃⌘Q) and Log Out *name*… (⇧⌘Q; ⌥⇧⌘Q without asking). Restart… and Shut Down… ask "Are you sure you want to restart your computer now?" with "Reopen windows when logging back in", Cancel and the default button, and do it by themselves after 60 seconds. Holding Option skips the question. Before it restarts, shuts down or logs out, every application is asked to quit (`applicationShouldTerminate:`), and one that refuses cancels the whole ("Pages canceled restart."). The power button offers Restart, Sleep, Cancel and Shut Down. The login window has Sleep, Restart and Shut Down at its foot.

## Decision

1. **The session is the one authority; logind is the one way to the machine.** Nothing in JavaScript, the shell or an application runs a command or speaks to logind. The program running the compositor (`oxyos-session`, and `oxyos-session --greeter` at the login screen) does, through its own part (`oxyos_compositor::session_end`), with a worker thread and a system bus connection of its own (`oxyos_power::ending`), never in the compositor's turn:
   - **Sleep** is logind's `Suspend(false)`, as ADR 0057's idle sleep: refused without asking when `CanSuspend` is not `yes`; the session's own sleep delay inhibitor (ADR 0010) puts the lock on screen first.
   - **Restart and Shut Down** are logind's `Reboot(false)` and `PowerOff(false)`: never interactive, so polkit decides without a prompt. `CanReboot` / `CanPowerOff` are asked first, before any application is asked to quit; anything but `yes` (`challenge`: another person's session is open, or a program's block inhibitor holds the machine and only an administrator may override it; `no`; `na`) **fails closed**: nothing is asked of the applications, nothing ends, and the person is told why (decision 6). logind applies the inhibitors; a delay inhibitor (a program saving) still delays.
   - **Log Out** ends the session as Settings' Sign Out does (ADR 0020, decision 5: `compositor.ending`), back to the login screen. Never at the login screen.
   - **Never in the development compositor's own machine.** `oxyos-offscreen` asks logind only on the played bus of a scenario (`--power-bus`, `oxyos_power::played`); without one every request is refused `unavailable`. No test, scenario or tool calls the system's logind's `PowerOff`, `Reboot` or `Suspend`.

2. **The menu bar's system menu** (the shell's Oxy menu, the top bar's first, as the Apple menu): System Settings…, Applications, Show Desktops, *separator*, **Sleep**, **Restart…**, **Shut Down…**, *separator*, **Lock Screen** (Ctrl+Super+Q, a Mac's ⌃⌘Q, the keymap's own), **Log Out *name*…** (Shift+Super+Q, ⇧⌘Q). *name* is the session's user's full name, else its user name. With Alt (Option) held while the menu is open, Restart…, Shut Down… and Log Out *name*… lose their ellipsis and their question, and Log Out shows Alt+Shift+Super+Q (⌥⇧⌘Q), as a Mac's alternates, the instant Option goes down or up: the compositor tells the surface with the keyboard the modifiers held as they change, and as it takes the keyboard with some held (`Modifiers`, event 169, protocol version 38, a Mac's `flagsChanged:`), which the host gives JavaScript as their own event (`OxyOSModifiersChanged`, the shell's `keyboard.subscribeModifiers`), so nothing waits for a key or a move; chosen so (or Option-clicked), they act at once. Spanish: *Reposo*, *Reiniciar…*, *Apagar equipo…*, *Bloquear pantalla*, *Cerrar sesión de nombre…*. Each row is a menu item named by its label, its chord said with it; the menu is Bloom's (ADR 0021), driven by the keyboard as every menu of the bar.

3. **The shell asks; the session decides.** Requests of the session's vocabulary (`SystemRequest`, the `shell` grant, each citing the press or key that asked; one citing none is refused `needsInput`, one while locked `locked`):

   | Request | Answer |
   | --- | --- |
   | `{"endSession": {"action": "logOut" \| "restart" \| "shutDown", "confirm": bool}}` | `{"confirming": {}}` (the question is shown, decision 4), `{"ending": {"action"}}` (the applications are being asked), or `{"refused": {"reason"}}`: `notPermitted` (logind's `Can…` is not `yes`), `unavailable` (no logind), `busy` (applications are being asked already), `locked`, `needsInput`, `invalidRequest` |
   | `{"sleep": {}}` | `{"sleeping": {}}` or `{"refused": {"reason"}}` |
   | `{"reopenWindows": {"enabled": bool}}` | `{"reopenWindows": {"enabled": bool}}`: the person's `reopen_windows` preference, kept (the checkbox remembers its state, as a Mac's) |
   | `{"powerKeyChoice": {"sheet": N, "choice": "restart" \| "sleep"}}` | as `endSession` with `confirm: false`, or `sleep`; only for the power key's sheet `N` while it is shown, which goes |

   A request for a new end while the question is shown replaces it (its sheet withdrawn). Super+Shift+Q and Super+Alt+Shift+Q are taken by the compositor as system shortcuts (decision 9, ADR 0024) and handed to the same part as `endSession` `logOut` with and without `confirm`; locked, as every system shortcut, they do nothing.

4. **The question and its countdown are the session's own system sheet** (ADR 0030's `SheetText`, drawn by the shell over everything but the lock screen, answered by the shell's press alone):
   - `{"kind": "end-session", "action": A, "seconds": 60, "reopenWindows": bool}`. *Are you sure you want to shut down your computer now?* / *…restart your computer now?* / *…quit all applications and log out now?*; the checkbox *Reopen windows when logging back in* (its state the preference's; toggled, `reopenWindows`); *If you do nothing, the computer will shut down automatically in 59 seconds.* (*…will restart automatically…*, *…you will be logged out automatically…*), counted down each second by the shell from `seconds`; **Cancel** and the default button **Shut Down** / **Restart** / **Log Out**: the keyboard starts on it, and Return presses it (Escape is Cancel). Spanish: *¿Seguro que quieres apagar el ordenador ahora?*, *¿Seguro que quieres reiniciar el ordenador ahora?*, *¿Seguro que quieres salir de todas las apps y cerrar la sesión ahora?*, *Volver a abrir las ventanas al volver a iniciar sesión*, *Si no haces nada, el ordenador se apagará automáticamente en 59 segundos.*, *Cancelar*, *Apagar*, *Reiniciar*, *Cerrar sesión*.
   - **The deadline is the session's**, not the shell's: 60 seconds after the sheet is shown the session withdraws it (`SheetDismissed`) and goes on as if accepted. A shell that crashed or hangs never shuts the machine down early, and never keeps it from the person's choice. The countdown goes on while locked, as a Mac's: the person chose and may have walked away.
   - **The screen reader** says the sheet as it appears (an alert dialog named by its question, its countdown and the checkbox's state as its description), then the focused default button; the countdown's text is not a live region, so it is not said every second.
   - **Reduce Motion**: nothing here moves; the sheet appears and goes without animation in every case.

5. **Every application is asked to quit first, and any may refuse** (`applicationShouldTerminate:`). Restart, Shut Down and Log Out, once confirmed and once logind says `yes`:
   - **Who is asked.** Every client that is an application: none of the `shell`, `lock`, `input_method`, `accessibility` or `screen_reader` grants. The shell, the lock, the input method and the screen reader end with the session.
   - **Protocol version 38** (ADR 0033): `QuitRequested { serial, reason }` (event 168; `reason` the `SessionEnd` wire enum: `LogOut` 1, `Restart` 2, `ShutDown` 3) to each application of version 38 or newer, and its answer `QuitReply { serial, quit }` (request 168). An application that answers `quit` then ends its own process; one that answers `cancel` cancels the whole end. A `QuitReply` naming no request the client was sent is ignored. An application of an older version cannot refuse: each of its windows is sent `CloseRequested` (as Super+Q quits it, ADR 0024), and it counts as having answered `quit`.
   - **The round.** The session asks every application at once and waits until each asked has answered `quit` and gone (its connection closed), or one answers `cancel`, at most `QUIT_DEADLINE` (30 s) from the asking. Then:
     - all gone: the end goes on (decision 1); what remains (an older application that kept running in the background, ADR 0060) is ended with the session (`Launcher::end`, SIGTERM then SIGKILL), or by systemd as the machine stops;
     - one cancelled: nothing more ends. Applications that already quit stay quit, as on a Mac. The person is told in a sheet: `{"kind": "end-session-cancelled", "action": A, "application": NAME}`, *“Pages” canceled restart.* (*canceled shut down.*, *canceled log out.*; *“Pages” ha cancelado el reinicio.*, *…el apagado.*, *…el cierre de sesión.*), with OK;
     - the deadline passed with one not answered or not gone: as cancelled, `{"kind": "end-session-timed-out", "action": A, "application": NAME}`, *Restart has timed out because the application “Pages” failed to quit.* *To try again, quit Pages and restart again.*
     `NAME` is the registry's name of the client's launch record (ADR 0013), else its application id.
   - **The application's side** (`@oxyos/platform`'s `application`, ADR 0058): `application.onQuitRequest(handler)`, the handler given the reason and answering `'quit'` or `'cancel'` (or a promise of one, after asking to save); several are asked in order, and any `'cancel'` cancels; a handler that throws cancels (an unsaved document is never lost to a bug). The Bloom host (`OxyOSApplication`) tells JavaScript `OxyOSQuitRequested` only while a handler is registered (`handlesQuit`); without one it answers `quit` at once. Answering `quit`, the host ends as for a close request, background activity or not. In the C ABI, `OXYOS_EVENT_QUIT_REQUESTED` (`code` the serial, `detail` the reason, `OXYOS_SESSION_END_*`), `oxyos_client_reply_to_quit` and `OXYOS_QUIT_REQUESTS_VERSION`.
   - **Reopen windows when logging back in.** With the preference on (the default, as a Mac's), the session writes the applications that ran when the end was asked (their registry ids, once each, in the order they were launched) to `$XDG_STATE_HOME/oxyos/reopen-at-login`, before asking them to quit; the next session (never the login screen, never Safe Mode) reads it once, removes it, and opens each one the registry still lists that is not already opened at login. A cancelled or timed-out end removes the file it wrote. Each application restores its own windows as it does when opened.

6. **What fails is said, and nothing half happens.** logind's `Can…` not `yes` (or no logind): the request is refused and, in a session, the sheet `{"kind": "end-session-failed", "action": A, "reason": R}` says *Your computer can't shut down now.* and why: `notPermitted`, *Someone else is logged in to this computer, or an application is keeping it on. An administrator must shut it down.*; `unavailable` / `failed`, *OxyOS couldn't reach the system's power service.* The call itself failing after the applications quit is said the same way; the session goes on, empty of them. Sleep refused: the same sheet with `action` `sleep`.

7. **The power key is the session's** (`KEY_POWER`, 116). The session holds logind's `handle-power-key` *block* inhibitor for its whole life, the login screen's included, so logind never turns the machine off at a press; the compositor takes the key as a hardware key (ADR 0025): before the system shortcuts, the input method and every application, press and release, locked or not, with any modifier held; a press is handed to the session part. What a press does follows logind's own policy (`HandlePowerKey`, read as the press comes), as a Mac's follows its own:
   - `poweroff` (logind's default), `reboot`, `halt`, `kexec` and anything else: **the choice**, never an abrupt power-off. In a session, the sheet `{"kind": "power-key", "reopenWindows": bool}`: *Are you sure you want to shut down your computer now?*, the checkbox, and **Restart**, **Sleep** at its foot's start, **Cancel** and **Shut Down** (the default). Restart and Sleep are `powerKeyChoice`; Shut Down goes on as decision 5 (no further question); no countdown. While locked, a press **sleeps** the machine (the lock is already on screen), as a locked Mac's. At the login screen, its own choice (decision 8);
   - `suspend`, `hibernate`, `hybrid-sleep`, `suspend-then-hibernate`, `sleep`: Sleep;
   - `lock`: the session locks;
   - `ignore`: nothing.
   A press while an end is being asked or questioned does nothing more. Holding the key is the firmware's (its forced off after several seconds) and logind's `HandlePowerKeyLongPress`, untouched.

8. **The login screen's Sleep, Restart and Shut Down**, at its foot, centred, as a Mac's login window: three round buttons with their glyph and their name under it (*Reposo*, *Reiniciar*, *Apagar*), each a button for the screen reader, reached with Tab. Pressed, they act at once: nobody is signed in, so there is nothing to confirm or ask, and the login screen's session (`--greeter`) asks logind as decision 1 says (`endSession` with `confirm: false`, `sleep`; `logOut` is refused there). A refusal is shown under the buttons. The power key at the login screen tells the login screen unasked (`SystemText` serial 0, `{"powerKey": {}}`), which shows the same choice as decision 7 in its own dialog (Restart, Sleep, Cancel, Shut Down; Escape cancels; Shut Down the default).

9. **The compositor's side** (`scene/session_end.rs`): `KEY_POWER` in `hardware_key`; Super+Shift+Q and Super+Alt+Shift+Q in `system_shortcut`; `take_session_keys` hands both over; the modifiers held told to the keyboard's surface as they change (`tell_modifiers`, version 38 and newer); `applications`, `ask_to_quit` (the event or the close requests by version), `QuitReply` checked against what was asked, and `take_quit_replies`. The scene decides nothing else: who quits, the deadline and logind are the session part's.

## Tests

- **Protocol:** `QuitRequested` and `QuitReply` round-trip, never sent to or taken from a connection of version 37 or older, `SessionEnd` a closed set, the documentation's table.
- **Scene** (`scene/tests/session_end.rs`): the power key's press and release reach no application, locked or not, with modifiers, and the press is handed over once; Super+Shift+Q and Super+Alt+Shift+Q handed over and reaching no one, nothing while locked; who is an application; a version 38 client sent `QuitRequested`, an older one `CloseRequested` for each toplevel; a reply taken once, only for what was asked, from the client asked.
- **logind** (`oxyos-power`, against the played logind): `Can…` mapped, `Reboot`/`PowerOff`/`Suspend` called once and non-interactive, refused when `Can…` is not `yes`, the inhibitor taken, `HandlePowerKey` read.
- **The session part** (`session_end/tests.rs`, played clock and logind): the question shown and the deadline going on by itself at 60 s, not before; Cancel; Option's at once; the round (all quit, one cancels and is named, one silent until the deadline, an old client's close requests); refused before any application is asked when logind says `challenge`; Log Out ending the session; the reopen file written and removed; the power key by each policy, locked and at the login screen; the greeter refusing `logOut`; nothing asked twice.
- **Shell, login screen and platform** (`apps/shell/tests`, `apps/login/tests`, `@oxyos/platform`'s tests): the menu's rows, order, separators, chords and alternates in both languages; the sheets' views; the countdown's text; the login screen's buttons; `onQuitRequest`'s answers.
- **Where.** `crates/oxyos-protocol` (`version_38_quit_requests_are_only_version_38s`), `crates/oxyos-compositor-core/src/scene/tests/session_end.rs`, `crates/oxyos-power/src/tests.rs` (`the_machine_restarts_and_shuts_down_through_logind_only_when_it_says_yes`), `crates/oxyos-compositor/src/session_end/tests.rs`, `crates/oxyos-client-ffi` (`a_request_to_quit_and_its_answer_cross_the_abi`), `apps/shell/tests/power.test.ts`, `apps/login/tests/power.test.ts`, `sample/oxyos/platform/tests/platform.test.ts`; the sample application `sample/QuitProbe.tsx`.
- **Scenarios** (`scenarios/session-end.json`, `session-end-scaled.json`, `session-end-logout.json`, `login-power.json`, over the played logind, never a real power-off): the menu's rows as the screen reader reads them; Restart… questioned, the keyboard on Restart, the application asked once (`delivered`), refusing, the sheet naming it and logind asked nothing; Shut Down… left 60 s and going on by itself, the application quitting, logind's `PowerOff false` once; a log out that completes (`session-end-logout`: the application asked, agreeing and gone, then `logged_out`, and logind seeing the session let the power key's inhibitor go); Option's Log Out at once, cancelled by an application with unsaved work; the Oxy menu's alternates following Alt down and up with no key or move after it (`Modifiers`); the power key's sheet and its Sleep (`Suspend false`); the login screen's Shut Down (`PowerOff false`) and its power key's choice.

## Consequences

- The machine is turned off, restarted and put to sleep only through logind as the person's session, with polkit's rules as they are: no command, no root, no prompt.
- An application with unsaved work is asked before anything ends, and can say no.
- The power key never turns the machine off abruptly while OxyOS runs.
