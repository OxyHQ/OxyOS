# ADR 0043: The genie

Status: **accepted; implemented**. Builds on [ADR 0011](./0011-transitions) (transitions, which this amends: its step 4's minimize and its "Not in this decision"'s genie), [ADR 0004](./0004-gpu-composition) (composition on the CPU and a Vulkan device, byte for byte), [ADR 0016](./0016-windows) (minimizing, and decision 10's next window), [ADR 0019](./0019-settings) (preferences and Settings), [ADR 0034](./0034-the-dock) (the dock) and [ADR 0033](./0033-protocol-versions) (version 20).

**5 November 2026: the genie only.** The platform's author decided that a window minimizes with the genie and nothing else: "efecto escala tiene que desaparecer y solo quedar efecto genie, no hace falta nada en settings de eso" (the Scale effect has to go and only the Genie effect stay; Settings needs nothing for it). So, as of this date:
- **The Scale effect is gone** (decision 6): the compositor has one way into the dock and out of it, the genie (`Shift::Dock` has no choice of effect, and `Transitions` none to set). A window the genie gives no mesh for (nothing to bend) is drawn in its slot, behind the dock's icon or past the output's edge, whole, as the genie leaves a window at its end.
- **The `minimize_effect` preference is retired** ([ADR 0019](./0019-settings)), as ADR 0040 retired `clock_24h`: `oxyos-preferences` neither reads, writes nor defaults it, and the session applies nothing for it. A preferences file that still has it (`genie` or `scale`, the ThinkPad's among them) is read whole, the line read as any unknown key is, and the next write leaves it out (`the_retired_minimize_effect_is_an_unknown_key_dropped_on_the_next_write`); a `SetPreference` naming it is refused as any unknown key is.
- **Settings has no Dock page:** "Minimise windows using" was its only content, so the page, its sidebar row and their strings (English and Spanish) are gone, and the sidebar is again the twelve rows it was before 4 November, Lock Screen followed by Displays. The Settings scenarios that the Dock row shifted (positions said, "1 de 13", rows counted, the sidebar's scroll, the views' numbers) expect that layout again, exactly as they did.
- **Reduce Motion's crossfade stays** (decision 7), as a Mac's Reduce Motion replaces the genie with a fade; Accessibility's description says so.
- **The protocol is unchanged.** Version 20's `ClearMinimizeTargets` and `PlaceMinimizeTarget` stay: the genie needs them to know where each window goes. Nothing in the protocol was the Scale effect's or its preference's alone: preferences travel as `key = value` text (`SetPreference`, `Preference`, ADR 0019), never as a message of their own, so no request or event is retired, and [ADR 0033](./0033-protocol-versions)'s add-only rule is kept without anything to stop sending or honouring.
- **The scenarios** `genie-scale.json`, `settings-dock.json` and `settings-dock-scaled.json` are gone from the tree and the deploy gate.

Decision 6 below is kept as it was decided, marked superseded; the rest stands.

## Problem

A window minimized on OxyOS shrank to a tenth of its size toward the dock's centre and faded (ADR 0011, step 4). ADR 0011 said why it was not a Mac's genie: "composition draws a surface as a rectangle, never bent." A Mac pours the window into its place in the Dock: the bottom edge narrows toward the slot while the top stays, then the window slides down the funnel the two sides make and under the Dock. The platform's author asked for the genie, faithfully, and pointed at [Raphael Salaja's *The Genie Effect*](https://www.raphaelsalaja.com/work/genie) (3 November 2026), which found the function the Dock runs for it.

Every rebuild before it drew the funnel by eye. Salaja loaded the Dock's machine code (macOS 27.0.1) into an emulator, fed it the record the Dock reads (the window's rectangle, the slot's, which edge the Dock is on, how far along), and matched the meshes it returned against a 120 fps recording of a real minimize and restore: 1 to 3 pixels on every frame. He then ported the function line by line, compared it with the emulator on 7,200 random windows (the Dock on all four sides, some windows overlapping it), every vertex bit for bit, and published the port in the page's code.

## Decision

1. **The mesh is the Dock's function, ported from Salaja's port** (`crates/oxyos-compositor/src/genie.rs`), not a curve drawn to look like it. In order:
   - the move is two phases, each eased by half a cosine, `ease(t) = 0.5 − 0.5·cos(πt)`: the sides bend toward the slot, then the window slides down between them;
   - each phase lasts `trunc(ln(d/2 + 1))·50`: for the bend, `d` is the larger of how far the sides move (summed, over 8) and twice any overlap with the Dock; for the slide, the travel;
   - they meet where the window's leading edge reaches the slot: `reach = slide·acos(1 − 2·gap/travel)/π`, `narrow = max(bend, reach)`, `lead = max(bend − reach, 0)`, `along = (slide + lead)·progress`, `bent = ease(clamp(along/narrow))`, `slid = ease(clamp((along − lead)/slide))`;
   - each side is a cosine from the window's edge to the slot's, from where the window's top is (the funnel's start) over a period of the travel, halved recursively until each piece is within 0.6 points of straight: no piece under 8 points, straightness checked only under 128, at most 64 splits;
   - rows that passed into the slot are clamped to its near edge, and a last row is added at the slot's far edge once the window has travelled further than the gap it started with;
   - the mesh is two columns wide (two rows tall with the Dock at a side), as many rows as the sides needed: 5, 10, 16 and 6 at 0, ¼, ½ and ¾ of the page's recorded minimize (an 800 × 500 window into a 35 × 55 slot on an 1800 × 1169 point display), its peak.

   Values are rounded to 32-bit floats wherever the Dock stores one, and its comparisons that choose between two values are kept as written (they differ from `min` and `max` for NaN and −0), as the port keeps them. All four edges are ported; OxyOS's dock is at the bottom (ADR 0009), the edge drawn.

2. **The thresholds are in points, and an OxyOS display's points are its logical pixels.** The 0.6-point tolerance, the 8- and 128-point lengths and the 50-point steps are the Dock's in points, so the mesh is worked out in the global space's logical pixels, whatever the display's scale, and each screen draws it at its own: at 1.25 every vertex is placed 1.25 times as far, the funnel the same shape as at 1. (Salaja's page scales its small desktop's mesh up from a 1280-point screen for the same reason: a display's pixels fed in would give another shape.)

3. **Progress is linear over half a second, on the frame clock; restoring runs the same mesh from 1 to 0.** The page fitted a straight line through the recorded progress: 495 ms to minimize, 497 to restore, within 0.21 % and 0.50 % of it; the two phases' easing is inside the function. So the window into the dock is not one of ADR 0011's springs: `Shift::Dock` in `transitions.rs`, 500 ms (`DOCK_DURATION`), its progress `elapsed/500` at each frame's time on the compositor's clock (`CLOCK_MONOTONIC`), composed at every refresh of the display while it runs (60 or 120 Hz, ADR 0011 decision 4, ADR 0018). Restored, the window comes back out of the same place along the same mesh, progress `1 − elapsed/500`. Into the dock and out of it are one mesh played either way, as Salaja drives his with one number: a window restored while it goes in turns back from where it is, and one minimized while it comes out goes back in from there.

4. **The compositor draws the window through the mesh itself, from its last frame.** A mesh warp is not a transform: every row of the window has its own width. `oxyos_gpu::Warp` is a layer's pixels drawn through rows of a mesh (`src/warp.rs`):
   - **each quad is filled bilinearly,** not as two triangles: Salaja filled a window with a gradient whose every pixel is its own position, recorded it minimizing, and read the positions back: two triangles were 4.33 and 3.96 pixels off (median, along each diagonal), a bilinear or projective fill 1.97, which is what the window server does near enough. The Dock's rows are horizontal (vertical at a side), so the bilinear map's inverse is exact and cheap: an output pixel's share down its quad is its height between the two rows, and across it the share between the two sides at that height; the source point is that far across the window's width and down its rows' heights;
   - **the source is sampled over the pixel's footprint** along each axis, never less than one source pixel (ADR 0011 step 5's box filter): a window squeezed into a 42-point slot is the average of what each output pixel covers, never dropped rows and columns; the mesh's edges are anti-aliased by the part of a pixel they cover; the window's rounded corners are its pixels' (`radius` in source pixels), so they bend with it;
   - **on the device and the CPU alike, byte for byte** (ADR 0004): the arithmetic is integer (sixteenths of a pixel, the share down a quad in 4096ths, 32 bits throughout), in `warp.rs` and repeated operation for operation in `compose.wgsl`, the compute shader every layer is composed with. The device finds each pixel's quad by a binary search of the rows, uploaded with each frame (a mesh changes every frame; 16 rows are 256 bytes). The CPU reference composes the same mesh with the same arithmetic: the fixture's frames are the device's. The owner asked for a vertex mesh filled in a fragment shader on Vulkan and Skia's `drawVertices` or an equivalent on the CPU. The composition is a compute pass, not a raster pipeline, and the fill here is what a fragment shader with the bilinear inverse would compute; `drawVertices` fills triangles affinely, which is the 4-pixel error, and a raster pipeline's interpolation is not specified to the bit, so neither would keep ADR 0004's equality between the two paths;
   - **from the window's last frame:** the compositor keeps the last frame it composed of every window (ADR 0011), draws the window going from it, and the client is not asked to draw anything. Its pixels keep their identity (`Snapshot::source`), so the Vulkan compositor draws them from where it holds them for the half second, never uploading them again. Restored, the window is drawn through the mesh from the frames it shows, as any window arriving is.
   - **beneath the shell's panels**, as a Mac's genie goes under the Dock: a window going into the dock is drawn under the first surface above every window (`Scene::above_windows`: the dock, the top bar, overlays, the lock), so the part of it that has passed into the slot is behind the dock. At progress 1 the mesh is the slot's rectangle, behind its icon: the window is gone into it, never popped off the screen.
   - **its shadow fades over the first tenth of the way** (50 ms; restored, the last tenth), at the window's own place: a Mac draws the window bent without one, and a shadow cast by a rectangle under a bent window would show where the window is not.

5. **Where the window goes is the shell's to say: its application's icon in the dock.** A Mac's Dock has a section of minimized windows by the Trash, unless *Minimize windows into application icon* is on. OxyOS's dock has no such section (ADR 0034: its applications, the Trash past a divider), so a window minimizes into its application's icon, as with that setting on. The compositor does not know where the shell lays the icon out, so the shell tells it, as Wayland's foreign-toplevel `set_rectangle` and Plasma's minimized geometry do: **protocol version 20** (ADR 0033), added only:

   | Direction | Opcode | Message |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Request | 92 | `ClearMinimizeTargets { surface }` (shell) |
   | Request | 93 | `PlaceMinimizeTarget { surface, window, rect }` (shell) |

   On one of its shell surfaces (the dock), the shell lists, by the window numbers it is told (ADR 0009), the rectangle (surface-local logical pixels) each window goes into; the list holds from the surface's next `Submit`, as ADR 0022's window views are sent (at most 256, `MAX_MINIMIZE_TARGETS`; a window placed again is where it was placed last). Only the shell, on its own shell surfaces (`Unauthorized`, `InvalidRole`; a target outside a list begun, `InvalidOrder`). `Scene::minimize_target` gives the rectangle in the global space. The Bloom host's `OxyOSMinimizeTarget` component sends its own frame for the windows it names, as `OxyOSWindowView` sends its; the shell's dock wraps each running application's icon in one with its windows. Without a target (a shell of an older version, a window the shell does not list), a window goes into a 42-point square in the dock's middle; without a dock, past the output's bottom edge's middle.

6. *Superseded on 5 November 2026 (above): the genie is the only effect, with no preference or Settings page.* **Settings › Dock: Minimise windows using Genie effect or Scale effect,** as a Mac's *Desktop & Dock › Minimize windows using*. The user preference `minimize_effect` (`genie`, the default, or `scale`), kept and told as every preference is (ADR 0019), applied by the session to the transitions (`Transitions::set_minimize_effect`) for windows minimized from then on. The page gives the Scale effect no mathematics, so it is the obvious affine one, with the genie's timing: the window's rectangle goes from where it is to the largest rectangle of its proportions within the slot, centred in it, eased by the Dock's half cosine over the same linear 500 ms; it stays whole until its last 30 % (150 ms) and fades out over it, eased the same way; restoring, the same backwards. Its shadow goes with it, scaled.

7. **Reduce Motion replaces the genie with a short crossfade,** as a Mac's Reduce Motion does: the window fades out where it is, and in where it is when restored (ADR 0011's `CROSSFADE`, 200 ms), nothing bent, scaled or moved.

8. **A window being minimized takes no input, and the keyboard moves at once.** The scene hides the window the moment it is minimized; only the compositor draws it as it goes, from its last frame. Every press, key or pointer over where it is drawn goes to what is beneath, never to it, and the keyboard goes to its application's next window at once (ADR 0016, decision 10), not when the genie ends.

## Tests

- **The mesh** (`genie/tests.rs`): the phases worked out by hand for the page's recording (bend 150 ms, slide 300, gap 474, reach 147.45, lead 2.55, the bend done at 0.4958 of the progress) and at six points of the progress; the page's row counts at its four points (5, 10, 16, 6); open, the window cut in pieces of 125 points; a quarter in, the sides the cosine from the window's corners to the slot's at every row, and the pieces halved to 31.25 near the top and 125 lower; three quarters in, the rows clamped to the slot's top and the last row at its bottom, in it at the end; every row in order at every hundredth. And against the page's own code (its published bundle's `genieMesh`, run under Node): 48 random minimizes with the Dock on each side and windows overlapping it, every vertex the same 32-bit float (`reference.json`); locally 7,200 the same way, the page's own check, every one of 626,408 vertices equal.
- **The warp** (`oxyos-gpu`): a rectangle drawn through itself is its pixels exactly (opaque, premultiplied, with corners); a quad filled bilinearly, a hand-worked pixel (row 32's pixel 31 between columns 30 and 31, 9/24 and 15/24: 123) and every row the source's whole width between the sides; a flat colour squeezed through a funnel stays exactly that colour, its edges that colour by the part covered; a mesh that cannot be drawn draws nothing; the frame file keeps it (`OXYFRAME5`). On Mesa's lavapipe the Vulkan composition is the reference byte for byte for funnels of 5, 16 and 40 rows, rows clamped to one height, at opacities 255, 128 and 7, with and without corners, a premultiplied layer, clipped and from damage.
- **The transitions:** linear over 500 ms (250 permille at 125 ms, 998 at 499), whole throughout, the mesh the window's rectangle at first and the slot's at the end, the shadow 255, 128 and 0 at 0, 25 and 50 ms; restored, the same mesh back (a quarter in at 375 ms is the minimize's at 125 ms); restored 150 ms into a minimize, back out from 300 permille, and minimized again 75 ms later, in from 150; a window the genie has no mesh for in its slot, whole; with reduce motion a crossfade where the window is. Through real sockets on the compositor's clock: a quarter in, where the window's top was is the desktop and the funnel's middle is the window, and every frame of it is within its damage.
- **The protocol and the scene:** the requests round-trip, are version 20's; only the shell, on its shell surfaces, in order, held from the next Submit; the global rectangle.
- **The C ABI and the host:** `oxyos_client_set_minimize_targets` sends the list whole and refuses a compositor of version 19; the Bloom host's `OxyOSMinimizeTarget` reports its frame on its surface, unclipped, for the windows it names (the same rules as `OxyOSWindowView`'s); the shell's dock gives each running application's icon its windows (`apps/shell/tests/dock.test.ts`).
- **Scenarios** (`regress.py`, the development compositor composing on the CPU, the gate's fixture), each run against the page's numbers, never against a recording of OxyOS:
  - `genie.json` (1280 × 720 at 120 Hz) and `genie-scaled.json` (1600 × 1000 at 1.25 and 60 Hz): GenieProbe's one-colour 800 × 500 window beside Inbox's. Its dock icon is where the window minimizes into (`minimize_target`). With the transitions' clock held (`--transition-clock hold`, then 125, 250 and 375 ms), the output's pixels at chosen points of the funnel a quarter, half and three quarters of the way, worked out from the page's own mesh code for this window and this icon: the window's colour in the funnel's middle at three heights, and anything else ten pixels past either side and where the window's top was once it has slid (the drawn sides are within a pixel of the page's mesh at every row, the rounded top corners aside). The window leaves the scene at once and the next window takes the keyboard in the step the genie begins; a press on the funnel reaches Inbox's window beneath it and nothing of the window going. At the end the window is gone into the icon and the icon still says Allo runs (its accessible value and its running mark's pixels). Then on the compositor's own clock, restored out of the icon and minimized into it again: every frame where a linear motion of 500 ms is at its time (`linear_ms`), one frame a refresh at most (`refresh_frames`, the least held only with `--speed-budgets`, #304).
  - `genie-reduce-motion.json`: with Reduce Motion, 100 ms into the minimize the whole window is half faded where it is, its corners too; restored, it stays still (`still`).

  Run with the scale effect (while it was there, 4 November 2026), `genie.json` failed at every point past a side; with Reduce Motion off, `genie-reduce-motion.json` fails. They are in the deploy gate; `shell-windows.json` checks the restore and minimize linear too.

## Measured

On the ThinkPad (i5-1240P), `perf.py genie --check`, 5 runs, isolated as #279 ran (a user and mount namespace with `/dev/dri` and the system OxyOS directories empty, so no GPU was opened, its own home, the CPU composing; the owner's session untouched): at 60 Hz and 1.25, the restore drew 31 frames and the minimize 30 of the 31 its 500 ms hold, every frame composed within its refresh (6.24 ms median, 14.66 at the 95th percentile, none late); at 120 Hz, 58 and 57 of 61, composing 3.99 ms median but 9.2 ms at the 95th percentile, over the 8.3 ms refresh: the first 50 ms, the shadow fading under the window, cost the most. The numbers are in `docs/performance.mdx`. The session composes on the Iris Xe; its 120 Hz is to be measured on a display that runs at it.

## Not in this decision

- **A live window as it goes.** The window is drawn from its last frame, as the page's copies are made when the window starts to move; whether macOS keeps a video playing in it is not known.
- **Minimized windows' own tiles in the dock**, a Mac's section by the Trash: the dock shows applications (ADR 0034).
- **The dock at a side or the top:** the mesh is ported for every edge; OxyOS's dock is at the bottom.
