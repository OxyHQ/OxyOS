# ADR 0039: Full Keyboard Access and the accessibility audit

Status: **accepted; implemented** (the host's keyboard focus and ring, the accessibility tree's modal rules, Super+F3 and Super+F8, the shell's and Settings' fixes, Bloom's). Builds on [ADR 0003](./0003-accessibility-protocol) (the accessibility tree), [ADR 0014](./0014-screen-reader) (the screen reader), [ADR 0024](./0024-system-shortcuts) (Super+F2, decision 9) and [ADR 0034](./0034-the-dock).

## Problem

A Mac can be used without a pointer. With Full Keyboard Access on, Tab and Shift+Tab move a focus ring through every control of the window, Space presses the control the ring is on, the arrows move a slider; Control-F2 reaches the menu bar, Control-F3 the Dock, Control-F8 the status menus; Escape closes what is on top and the keyboard goes back where it came from; a sheet holds the keyboard until it is answered. VoiceOver's cursor follows the keyboard's focus and reads each control by its name, value, state and role.

OxyOS had only part of it. The menu bar was reachable (Super+F2, ADR 0024) and Bloom's menus and popups drove themselves from the keyboard (ADR 0021, `floating/popup-navigation.tsx`); a text field could be focused by a press. Nothing else could be reached from the keyboard: no Tab between controls, no ring, no way to press a button, no way into the dock, the status items, Control Center or the Notification Center, and only the system sheet and Launchpad took the keyboard at all. The accessibility tree also published the window behind an open modal and behind a view marked modal, so a screen reader could leave a dialog without closing it.

## Decision

1. **Keyboard focus is the platform's, as Android's keyboard navigation and a Mac's key view loop are** (`tools/rn-host-spike/keyboard-focus.h`). The Bloom host keeps one focused control per client, beside the text field it already focused:
   - **What is focusable:** a view React Native's Android view would let the keyboard focus (`focusable`, which `Pressable` and the touchables set) that is also an accessible element, enabled, shown (not transparent, not `pointerEvents: none`, not hidden from assistive technologies) and of some size; an editable text input. A backdrop pressed to close a panel (`accessible={false}`) is no stop, as VoiceOver and a Mac's key view loop go through the same controls.
   - **Where Tab goes:** through the focusable views of the surface that has the keyboard, in reading order (each parent's children in order, as the accessibility tree reads them), round the ends; Shift+Tab back. The surface's content is the window's, or the topmost modal's while one is shown, or the topmost view marked modal for assistive technologies (`accessibilityViewIsModal`, a Bloom overlay's and the system sheet's root): a modal holds the keyboard as it holds the screen reader. A popup's keys stay its own (Bloom's popup navigation, where Tab closes a menu).
   - **What acts:** Space and Return press the focused control (React Native's accessibility click, which Pressability answers with `onPress`, as an assistive technology's activation); the arrows step an adjustable control that declares increment and decrement. The focused control has these keys before the application's own, as a Mac's first responder has them before the window: a list's Space (Stash's Quick Look) never takes a toolbar button's. Tab always moves focus; no text field types a tab.
   - **What is told:** the control left receives `onBlur` and the one reached `onFocus` (React Native's focus events, `BaseViewEventEmitter`); a text input reached by Tab is focused through the text editing with its whole text selected, as a Mac's field is. The assistive technology's focus follows (the tree marks the control focused), so the screen reader says each control once as Tab reaches it, as VoiceOver's cursor follows the keyboard's. While the keyboard is navigating, the application's own accessibility focus moves (Bloom giving a closed menu's or dialog's trigger the focus back) move the keyboard's too.
   - **The ring:** drawn by the host around the focused control while the keyboard moved it, as a Mac draws its keyboard focus ring: a band of the system blue at 55%, 3 pixels wide, a pixel off the control's edge and following its corners, over the control and within what clips it (`TextInputEdit::focusRing`, `kFocusRingReach`; damage covers it, `damage.cpp`). It shows only while the surface it is in has the keyboard (or a popup of it, a menu opened from it), never in a window or a panel the keyboard left. A press of the pointer hides it and takes focus off a control that is no text field, as Android's touch mode hides its focus highlight.
   - **Modals:** a modal coming over the control with the keyboard remembers it; while the ring shows, the keyboard moves to the modal's first control, as a Mac's sheet takes it. The modal leaving gives the keyboard back to the control it came from. A focused control that goes takes focus with it.
   - **Scroll views** bring the focused control into view, as Android's `requestChildFocus` does.

2. **The accessibility tree hides what a modal covers.** While a modal is shown the window's tree is the topmost modal's alone (Android's dialog window is all TalkBack reads); a view marked `accessibilityViewIsModal` is all of its surface's tree (as VoiceOver reads it). A navigation landmark (`role="navigation"`, a sidebar's sections) is published as a group of its controls, as VoiceOver reads it, not as a nameless generic node.

3. **The dock and the status items from the keyboard: Super+F3 and Super+F8,** as a Mac's Control-F3 and Control-F8, with Super for Control as Super+F2 is (ADR 0024, decision 9). Protocol version 12 adds `SystemShortcut::Dock` (9) and `StatusMenus` (10) to the shortcuts the compositor sends the shell of the active window's output; a client of version 11 or older is never sent them (`event_since`), as its reader would refuse a value outside its set. A menu open in a window closes first; the window stays active.
   - The shell's regions ask for the keyboard as the top bar does (`keyboard`), and say where its focus goes once they have it (`focusTarget`, `"first"` or a control's testID; each new `focusRequest` asks again). Of several regions asking, the newest has it (a sheet over the Control Center).
   - **The dock:** Super+F3 puts the keyboard on its first icon with the ring; ← → go along it; ↑ opens the focused icon's menu, as a Mac's Dock (Bloom's context menu, driven from the keyboard); Space and Return open it; Escape gives the window the keyboard back.
   - **The status items:** Super+F8 puts the keyboard on the first (the network's item, else the search); ← → go along them; Space and Return open one; Escape leaves them.
   - **Control Center, the Notification Center and the overview** have the keyboard while open, as a Mac's status menus and Mission Control do: Tab goes through their controls, Escape closes them, and the keyboard goes back to the window, or to the status items they opened from. Control Center opened from the keyboard starts on its first control, and moving between its modules and Wi-Fi's view keeps the keyboard in it.

4. **System sheets** are alert dialogs (`alertdialog`, named by their title, which is not read again) and say themselves as they appear: their title, what went wrong (a wrong password), and what they say, once; no live region says the error again. Return is still no answer (ADR 0030, decision 9), but the answer's button is reached with Tab and pressed with Space, as any control is: a consent that only a pointer could give would shut out everyone who cannot use one.

5. **The screen reader says a focused control changed in place** (ADR 0014): a switch or a box toggled by Space or a press says its new state ("activado"), a slider stepped by the arrows its new value, once; a trigger's expanded state is said by the menu it opens, never as a word before it.

6. **Live changes said once:** Wi-Fi joined ("Conectado a Vecinos", from Control Center by the shell, from Settings by Settings, politely, once as it connects); a join that failed in Settings (its note's title and why); a sheet as it appears; a notification's banner (as before).

7. **Bloom** (15.92.0, OxyHQ/Bloom#590): a dialog's panel and a named centred card carry `role="dialog"`; a grouped section's row reads its value with its name, and a row without a press is one element (its title and value) or, holding a control of its own, no element at all, never a role-less container whose title is read again inside it; a select's trigger reads its chosen value; a meter ring says how far along it is as a share when its caller gives no words.

## Audit (1 November 2026)

Every surface was walked with the screen reader's tree and with the keyboard alone, against VoiceOver and Full Keyboard Access: role, name, state and value of each control; focus order, its ring, Tab and Shift+Tab; the menu bar and dock shortcuts; Escape and where focus returns; modal sheets; live changes; double announcements; hidden content. Fixed in this decision unless marked *remaining*.

- **Everywhere (host):** no Tab traversal, ring or keyboard activation; the window behind a modal and behind a view marked modal stayed in the tree; `navigation` read as generic. *Remaining:* a popup that is not a menu (a popover with fields) is not traversed by Tab; `accessibilityHint` and `aria-describedby` are not published (the protocol has no field for help); `accessibilityValue`'s minimum and maximum are not turned into a share; live regions on views that are not elements (Bloom's toasts) are not followed; there is no collection count ("3 of 12").
- **Top bar:** the status items could not be reached (Super+F8). *Remaining:* the clock is named "Centro de notificaciones" without the date and time it shows, and the network, Control Center and clock items carry no expanded state (Bloom's `Button` passes no `accessibilityValue` or state of the caller's); the battery item has no role.
- **Menus, the dock's menu, grouped toolbars, text editing** (#226, #231, #236, #243): verified, unchanged.
- **Control Center:** no keyboard, no Escape, no focus moved in; Wi-Fi joined not said. *Remaining:* its panel's role is `menu` though it holds no menu items; each level card reads twice (its adjustable wrapper and Bloom's slider inside); the switch modules' status text is read after the switch said it; "Redes Wi-Fi" does not say the network joined.
- **Notifications:** the Notification Center had no keyboard or Escape. *Remaining:* no shortcut opens it; each card's close button is "Borrar" alone; a banner leaves after five seconds even while focused or replied to by the keyboard.
- **Dock:** no keyboard entry, no arrows, menus only by long press, secondary or Control-click (Super+F3, ↑). *Remaining:* icons are named "Abrir X" (verb first) and say nothing of running or not responding; the Trash does not say whether it is empty; Bloom's `ContextMenuTrigger` declares no accessibility action to open its menu.
- **Launchpad:** *remaining:* the arrow keys' highlight is neither focus nor said; with a folder open, the grid behind stays in the tree; opening and the number of results are not said; the page dots do not say the total.
- **Overview and switcher:** the overview had no keyboard and Escape never reached it. *Remaining:* each window is two buttons (its title and its card) doing the same; moving a window to another desktop is drag-only; the current desktop is not marked; the switcher's chosen name is read after the button said it.
- **Lock screen and login:** *remaining:* the throttle countdown is a live region that changes every second; the hint is both an alert and a live region; locking says nothing; the login's account chooser is not reachable by arrows and there is no way to turn the screen reader on before signing in.
- **System sheets:** an alert, not a dialog; the title read twice; nothing said as one appeared; the error at once a live region and an alert. *Remaining:* the shell shows no install progress.
- **Screenshots:** *remaining:* the thumbnail opens only by gestures; options are buttons, not radios; the interface's opening and countdown are silent; its toolbar is the compositor's keys, not Tab.
- **Settings:** accents were named by Bloom's text-colour flag (yellow read "white") and in English; every per-application Update button said only "Update"; Wi-Fi joined and a failed join were silent; static rows read their title twice and never their value (Bloom); selects never said their choice (Bloom). *Remaining:* sidebar group labels are not headings and the sidebar is one Tab stop per row rather than one with arrows; the "Saved" toast stays in the tree while invisible; the Displays trial dialog does not take focus and Escape does not revert; an arranged display cannot be moved from the keyboard; Software Update has no installing state; Privacy's switches are named by capability alone; segmented controls and radio groups have arrow keys only on the web (Bloom).
- **Stash:** the toolbar and the sidebar are reached and pressed by Tab and Space, which its list's Space no longer takes. *Remaining:* its items are no Tab stop and moving the selection with the arrows says nothing of the item; its context menu cannot be opened from the keyboard; a row reads its name without its kind, date and size; transfers' progress toasts are not said.
- **Atlas:** *remaining:* Get unmounts into a progress ring and Open replaces it, losing focus and saying nothing; finished updates vanish without a word; the search's result count is not said; no keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+F, Ctrl+[).

## Tests

- **Host scenarios** at 1 and 1.25, each step checking the assistive technology's tree, what it would say (exact text and count) and the keyboard's focus (`launched` trees for an application the session started, `regress.py`):
  - `keyboard-settings`, `keyboard-settings-scaled`: Settings opened from the Oxy menu with Super+F2 and Return, Tab through its sidebar to Accessibility, Space, Tab to Reduce Motion and Space: the preference changes, every control said once as Tab reaches it.
  - `keyboard-wifi`, `keyboard-wifi-scaled`: Super+F8 to the network's item, Space opens Control Center at Wi-Fi's view on its first control, Tab to Vecinos, Space, the password sheet said as it appears, the password typed, Tab round the sheet's reveal button, Cancel and Join and back to its field (never out of the sheet), Return: "Conectado a Vecinos" said once, the daemon holding it.
  - `keyboard-dock`, `keyboard-dock-scaled`: Super+F3 on the dock's first icon, → to Inbox, Space launches it; Super+F3 again, → to Inbox, ↑ opens its menu, End and Return quit it; Escape gives Allo the keyboard back.
  - `keyboard-stash`, `keyboard-stash-scaled`: Tab and Shift+Tab through Stash's sidebar and toolbar, each control focused and said in reading order, Space pressing a sidebar row.
- **Scene:** `super_f3_and_super_f8_move_the_keyboard_to_the_dock_and_the_status_items`. **Protocol:** `version_12_shortcuts_are_never_sent_to_an_older_client`, the closed set. **Reader** (`the_focused_element_changed_in_place_says_its_new_state_or_value_once`) and **speech** (`a_focused_element_changed_in_place_says_its_new_state`): a switch turned on by Space says "activado" once, a slider stepped says its value, a trigger opening its menu says nothing before the menu's title.

## Not in this decision

- A preference to turn Full Keyboard Access off (it is always on, as on a Mac with the setting on); the ring's colour following the accent (it is the system blue); Ctrl+F4 to F7 (next window, toolbar, floating window, input fields only).
- The remaining items of the audit above.
