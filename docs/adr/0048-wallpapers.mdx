# ADR 0048: Wallpapers

Status: **accepted; implemented** (7 November 2026). Builds on [ADR 0009](./0009-session-shell) (the shell, the desktops), [ADR 0010](./0010-lock-screen) (the lock screen), [ADR 0012](./0012-solar-wallpaper) (the sun, which this amends), [ADR 0018](./0018-several-displays) (each display's identity), [ADR 0019](./0019-settings) (the preferences, which this amends: `wallpaper`), [ADR 0028](./0028-file-access) (the file chooser), [ADR 0031](./0031-gpu-buffers) and [`performance.mdx`](../performance) (the hosts' memory), [ADR 0033](./0033-protocol-versions) (version 29), [ADR 0039](./0039-keyboard-access) (the keyboard and the screen reader) and [ADR 0040](./0040-language-and-region) (the session's language).

## Problem

The desktop's picture cannot be changed. The shell shows one wallpaper, *Solar Gradients*, the folder `OXYOS_WALLPAPER` names, at the frame that shows the sun now (ADR 0012); without it, frame 8 of the same wallpaper, bundled into the shell. Settings has a Wallpaper page with one menu whose only entries are "OxyOS" and that wallpaper, and its preference, `wallpaper`, is kept and never read.

A Mac's System Settings › Wallpaper shows the current wallpaper at its top with its name and, for a dynamic one, a menu of Automatic and its variants (Tahoe's Day, Morning, Evening, Night); *Show on all Spaces*; then the system's wallpapers by category, each a row of thumbnails with *Show All (N)*, the chosen one outlined; shuffles of a category with how often; the person's photos and folders (*Add Photo…*, *Add Folder…*), each with how it fits the screen; and colours, with a colour of the person's own. A choice applies at once, with a crossfade, on the display whose settings window made it, to the current Space or to every Space, and the lock screen shows it.

And *Solar Gradients* is not OxyOS's to ship. It was imported from a HEIC the owner supplied (`assets/wallpapers/solar-gradients/README.md`: "No ownership or relicensing of the supplied artwork is asserted"); its name, its 5120 × 2880 frames and its `apple_desktop:solar` metadata are those of the dynamic wallpaper Apple ships with macOS. The repository is public, and the shell's package bundles frame 8: OxyOS distributes it.

## Decision

1. **The system's wallpapers are a catalog in the image**, `/usr/share/oxyos/wallpapers` (`OXYOS_WALLPAPERS` names another, for development and scenarios: the repository's copy is `config/includes.chroot_after_packages/usr/share/oxyos/wallpapers`, which the image installs). It is read by `crates/oxyos-wallpaper`, never written.
   - **`catalog.json`:** the categories in the order Settings shows them (an id, and a name in each of the session's languages), the catalog's `default` wallpaper, and the solid colours offered (`#rrggbb` and a name each).
   - **A wallpaper is a folder,** its name its id (lowercase letters, digits and dashes), with `wallpaper.json`: its name, its category, its variants (each an id, a name and its file: SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP, in the folder), the variant shown as its preview, and how *Automatic* chooses among them: `none` (one variant), `appearance` (each variant `light` or `dark`: the session's appearance, ADR 0019, itself automatic or not), or `sun` (each variant the sun's elevation and azimuth it shows: the variant closest to the sun now where the system's time zone is, ADR 0012's rule).
   - **What each is, and whose,** is recorded as the image records its sounds: `/usr/share/doc/oxyos-wallpapers/copyright`. A wallpaper with no provenance and licence there is not shipped.
2. **The person's choice is one value** (`oxyos_preferences::wallpaper::Choice`), at most 1100 bytes:
   - empty: the catalog's default;
   - `NAME`, or `NAME/VARIANT`: a system wallpaper, *Automatic* or a fixed variant;
   - `color:RRGGBB`: a solid colour;
   - `photo:FIT:RRGGBB:PATH`: a photo of the person's, `FIT` one of `fill`, `fit`, `stretch`, `center`, `tile` (a Mac's Fill Screen, Fit to Screen, Stretch to Fill Screen, Centre Image and Tile), and the colour of what `fit`, `center` and `tile` leave uncovered;
   - `folder:FIT:RRGGBB:EVERY:PATH`: the photos of a folder of the person's, in turn;
   - `shuffle:CATEGORY:EVERY`: a category's wallpapers in turn (`colors`, the catalog's colours);
   - `EVERY` is `login` (a new one each time the session starts) or minutes, 1 to 10080 (Settings offers 5, 15, 30, 60 and 1440).

   A path is absolute, at most 1024 bytes, UTF-8 with no control character, no `.` or `..` component and no `/` at its end. It is the file or folder the person chose in the file chooser (ADR 0028), read as the person reads it; nothing is copied.
3. **The choice is kept for each display, and for each desktop when chosen,** in the preferences file (ADR 0019), as a Mac keeps a wallpaper for each display and Space:
   - `wallpaper = CHOICE`: the default, which a display with no choice of its own shows;
   - `wallpaper.DISPLAY = CHOICE`: a display's own, by its identity (ADR 0018, decision 10);
   - `wallpaper.DISPLAY.N = CHOICE`: desktop `N`'s on that display (counted from 1, as Mission Control names them), while *Show on all desktops* is off;
   - `wallpaper_all_desktops = true|false` (on by default), a preference as any other.

   **A choice is made where Settings is**, on the display that holds most of its window, on the active desktop, as a Mac's settings window sets the wallpaper of the display it is on. On the primary display (ADR 0018: the first, which has the dock) it is the default; on another, that display's own. Before the default changes, every display shown with no choice of its own is given the old default as its own, so a choice changes only the display it was made on. While *Show on all desktops* is off, a choice is the active desktop's on that display alone. Turned on, every display keeps what it shows on its active desktop, on every desktop, and every desktop's choice goes. A desktop removed takes its choices with it, and those after it follow their desktop's new number. Displays and desktops' choices are kept for at most 16 displays, the one chosen last at the end, as the displays' settings are.

   What a display shows on a desktop is its desktop's choice, else its own, else the default.
4. **Settings sees and sets one value, `wallpaper`: the choice where its window is.** The session tells Settings `wallpaper` as the choice shown on Settings' display and active desktop (`Preference`, ADR 0019), and again when its window moves to another display, the active desktop changes, or any choice there does. `wallpaper=CHOICE` from Settings is judged against decision 2's grammar and applied there by decision 3's rules; the per-display and per-desktop lines are the session's, never asked for. `wallpaper_all_desktops` is set as any preference.
5. **The session tells each display's shell what its display shows** (`OutputWallpaper`, event 82, protocol version 29, to shell clients: an output and its choice's text in chunks): for every output when the shell connects, and whenever what an output shows changes (a choice, the active desktop, a display added). Each shell takes its own output's. The login screen's session, which has no person's preferences, shows the catalog's default.
6. **The shell's host turns the choice into a picture** (`oxyos_wallpaper_show`, `crates/oxyos-wallpaper` through the C ABI), at the moment and in the appearance the session gives it:
   - a system wallpaper's variant, *Automatic* chosen by decision 1's rule, and when that next changes (the sun reaching another variant, ADR 0012; the appearance, when the session tells it);
   - a shuffle's or a folder's current one: the time is cut into slots of `EVERY` minutes counted from the Unix epoch, and the slots into rounds of as many slots as there are candidates (a folder's images by name; a category's wallpapers in the catalog's order); each round shows every candidate once, in an order shuffled from a hash of the round, the display and the choice, its first never the one the round before ended on. `login` is one pick for the session, from when it started. So a shuffle needs no state, never shows the same picture twice in a row, and each display shuffles on its own;
   - what to draw: the file, how it fits, and the colour behind it.

   The shell draws it with the platform's one image pipeline: a raster decoded at the size it is drawn at (a JPEG at a power-of-two reduction from its coefficients, `oxyos-image`), an SVG drawn by Skia at that size, so no wallpaper is held larger than its display. When it changes, the new one fades in over the old once it has been drawn (0.6 s; 2 s for one the clock brought: a variant, a shuffle), the old one dropped then. Reduce Motion keeps the crossfade, as ADR 0011 keeps fades. The lock screen and the login screen show the same picture, dimmed (ADR 0010).
7. **Settings › Wallpaper is a Mac's** (`apps/settings`, the page `wallpaper` and its sidebar item):
   - **at its top** the current wallpaper's preview and name, and its options: a dynamic wallpaper's menu (*Automatic*, then its variants); a photo's or folder's fit and the colour behind it; a shuffle's or folder's interval; a colour's own colour;
   - **Show on all desktops;**
   - **the categories**, each a row of thumbnails with *Show All (N)*, which shows them all, and their shuffle; the chosen one outlined in the accent;
   - **Your photos:** *Add Photo…* and *Add Folder…*, which open the system's file chooser through `expo-file-system`'s pickers (ADR 0028), and those added before, which Settings keeps in its own data (`Paths.document`);
   - **Colours,** and a colour of the person's own.

   A thumbnail is drawn from a cache (`oxyos_wallpaper_thumbnail`): each photo decoded once at the thumbnail's pixel size and kept as a PNG in `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/oxyos/wallpapers/thumbnails`, by its path, size, time and the thumbnail's size (at most 512 kept, the oldest going first); an SVG is drawn at its size and needs none. Thumbnails and a folder's images are read for Settings alone, the client the session granted `settings` (ADR 0019): they reach the person's files outside the files API (ADR 0028), so any other client asking is answered nothing.

   **The keyboard and the screen reader** (ADR 0039): every control is a keyboard stop in reading order; each row of thumbnails is one stop, a radio group, whose arrows move the choice and choose it, each thumbnail named by its wallpaper and said as chosen; Show All, the shuffles and the buttons are buttons; the header's controls say what they set. Every word is in English and Spanish (ADR 0040).
8. **OxyOS ships its own wallpapers.** *Solar Gradients* is removed from the repository and from the shell's package; ADR 0012's sun keeps choosing among the variants of OxyOS's own dynamic wallpaper. What ships is made for OxyOS from nothing but files of the repository (SVG gradients written for it, in Bloom's colours), recorded as such. Photographs and aerial videos come only from the owner, or under a licence recorded with them. The SVGs write every gradient in the drawing's own coordinates (`userSpaceOnUse`): the Skia revision the Bloom host draws with gives an `<ellipse>` no bounding box, so a gradient in its bounding box's units draws black there ([#346](https://github.com/OxyHQ/OxyOS/issues/346), a platform bug any application's SVG would hit, to be fixed in Skia).

## Results

- **The choice and where it is kept** (`crates/oxyos-preferences/src/wallpaper.rs`): every form round-trips and every malformed one is refused (a path with `..`, `.`, a doubled or trailing `/`, a control character, longer than 1024 bytes; an interval of 0 or past a week); a choice changes only the display it was made on (another display keeps the old default as its own); each desktop its own while *Show on all desktops* is off; a desktop removed takes its choice and renumbers the rest; turned on, each display keeps what it shows; the file's lines read back the same and bad ones are nothing.
- **The catalog and what a display shows** (`crates/oxyos-wallpaper`): the catalog read strictly (an unknown key, a file outside its folder, a variant its rule cannot use: that wallpaper is nothing, said); *Automatic* by the appearance and by the sun (Madrid at 03:00 on the June solstice is Horizon's night, noon its day; no place, its preview); what names nothing shows the default; photos' fits and margin colours, percent-encoded URIs; a shuffle never twice the same in a row, the same within its slot, each display on its own, at login one for the session; a thumbnail made once at its size and cropped as `cover`; the image's own catalog whole, every name in Spanish too.
- **Protocol, scene and session**: `OutputWallpaper` round-trips and is never sent to a shell of version 28; each shell is told each output's choice after its `Output`, the default as one empty chunk, and only what changed; Settings' display is its topmost window's; through the session (`settings.rs`), a choice made with Settings showing no window is the primary's, a long path arrives in chunks, a bad choice is refused, a second desktop gets its own, switching desktops tells the shell with no change of preference, and the file holds it all and reads back the same in a new session. The C ABI's declarations match the header.
- **Scenarios** (each at 1 and 1.25): `wallpaper-choice` (a colour, a photo filling, fitted and stretched, a picture centred and tiled, their margins exact), `wallpaper-desktops` (each desktop its own, both ways), `wallpaper-lock` (the lock screen shows the choice dimmed), `wallpaper-crossfade` (Dark Mode from the Control Center fades the OxyOS wallpaper's dark variant in), `solar-wallpaper` (Horizon's night at 03:00 in Madrid); `settings-wallpaper` (by pointer and by keyboard: a wallpaper, a variant, colours, a colour of one's own, all desktops off; the events delivered, the focus, the accessible tree, the file and the desktop's pixels), `settings-wallpaper-photos` (Add Photo… through Dropsea's chooser, the photo filling the desktop, Fit to Screen over white), `settings-wallpaper-folder` (Add Folder…, its tile and count, every five minutes, the desktop one of its photos).
- **The same pixels whatever was repainted** (8 November 2026): a blur over the wallpaper (Launchpad's backdrop, expo-blur's views) is worked out reduced on a grid anchored at its view, not at the part of it a frame repaints, so a pixel of it is the same whichever damage drew it; over the old, nearly flat wallpaper that never showed, over a gradient it was a level or three. And the scenarios' pixels are taken from a host built as the deploy builds it, React Native from Bloom's own (`rn-toolchain.sh`): one built against another React Native drew a launcher icon's corner a level or three apart.
- **What it costs**: an SVG wallpaper is drawn once at its display's pixels (1920 × 1200: 9 MiB), the crossfade holds two for 0.6 s; a photo is decoded at the display's size as before (a 5120 × 2880 JPEG reduced from its coefficients).

## Not in this decision

- Video wallpapers (aerials), until the platform decodes video; a Mac's screen saver.
- A wallpaper taken from a photo library (Dropsea's albums) rather than a file or folder.
- Each desktop's wallpaper drawn in Mission Control's desktop cards (ADR 0022 draws them without).
- The person's photos on the login screen, which shows the system's default: it runs before anyone signs in.
